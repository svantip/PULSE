TRAINING OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Two transformer models were trained and compared:
1. BERT (bert-base-multilingual-cased)
   - 12 layers, 12 attention heads, 768 hidden size
   - 110M parameters
   - Full BERT architecture

2. DistilBERT (distilbert-base-multilingual-cased)
   - 6 layers, 12 attention heads, 768 hidden size
   - 66M parameters (40% smaller)
   - Distilled from BERT (faster, retains 97% performance)

Both models used gradient-based optimization:
- Optimizer: AdamW with learning rate 2e-5
- Training: Backpropagation through transformer layers
- Loss: Weighted Cross-Entropy (class-balanced)

MODEL ANALYSIS & COMPARISON
----------------------------------------------------------------------

🏆 WINNER: DistilBERT Model
Performance advantage: 3.24%

Why DistilBERT Matches or Exceeds BERT:

1. EFFICIENT ARCHITECTURE:
   - Knowledge distillation retained most of BERT's capabilities
   - 40% smaller, 60% faster, while maintaining 97% performance
   - Better regularization may prevent overfitting

2. DATASET SIZE:
   - With 20K samples, smaller model may generalize better
   - BERT's extra capacity not fully utilized
   - Less prone to overfitting on limited data

3. TRAINING EFFIergence due to fewer parameters
   - Better gradient flow through 6 layers
   - More stable training dynamics

Benefits:
   - 2x faster inference (important for production)
   - 2x less memory (can run on smaller GPUs)
   - Equal or better performance
   - ✅ Recommended for deployment
